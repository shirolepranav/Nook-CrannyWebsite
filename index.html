Questions

Does it need Apple Intelligence?

No. Every feature works without it. Where your device supports it, it just saves you some typing by suggesting names.

Is there an account? Does it need internet?

No account and no internet are needed. The only things that use the internet are buying Pro and iCloud Sync, both through Apple.

What’s free and what’s Pro?

Free includes unlimited rooms, search, backups, up to 25 items and 3 warranty reminders. Pro is a single $14.99 purchase that unlocks unlimited items and reminders, the insurance PDF and CSV export, and iCloud Sync. It works with Family Sharing.

How do I move my home to a new iPhone?

Set up the new iPhone from your old one or a backup, turn on iCloud Sync with Pro, or save a backup file to Files. Backups are free.

Can I keep some items private?

Yes. Private items need Face ID or Touch ID to open and never appear in Spotlight, Siri or widgets. You can also lock the whole app.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone with iOS 27 or an iPad with iPadOS 27.
